## Why sfxpack?

Working with sound effects in game development often involves repetitive, time-consuming tasks. You’ve got a folder of WAVs, but Unity wants OGG and Godot prefers OGG or even WAV, each with specific import settings. Before that, you’re manually normalizing loudness to ensure a consistent audio experience, then meticulously trimming silence from the beginning and end of each file. This isn't just tedious; it's a bottleneck that pulls focus from creative work.

**sfxpack** automates this entire pipeline. It streamlines your workflow by taking a raw folder of WAVs and, in one command, converts them to the correct formats for your chosen engine, ensures consistent loudness through LUFS normalization, and trims unnecessary silence. This means less time spent on mundane audio preparation and more time focused on making your game sound great.
